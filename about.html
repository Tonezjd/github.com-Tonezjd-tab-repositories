About Volta Power Electric

Owner-Operated. Personally Certified.

Founded in 2025 and based in Rosemead, CA, Volta Power Electric was built on one idea: bring modern electrical expertise — EV, battery backup, Smart Panels — to Southern California homes and businesses, backed by real certifications and personal accountability.

Photo Placeholder — Abel Chavira, Founder & Owner
Meet the Founder

Abel Chavira

Abel founded Volta Power Electric to give Southern California homeowners and businesses an electrician who is personally trained on the technology shaping the future of the home and workplace — EV charging, battery backup, and smart, circuit-level electrical panels.

Abel is a UEI College certified electrical technician, OSHA 10 certified for jobsite safety, a SPAN Authorized Installer for Smart Panel systems, and a Tesla Certified Installer for Powerwall and EV charging equipment. Every Volta Power Electric job is backed by that training — and by Abel's direct, hands-on involvement.
